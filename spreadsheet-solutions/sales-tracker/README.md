# Sales Tracker

A simple business sales and expense tracking solution.

## Overview

Sales Tracker aims to help small businesses record daily transactions, monitor expenses, and evaluate their financial performance.

## Planned Features

- Daily sales records.
- Expense tracking.
- Revenue calculations.
- Profit calculations.
- Monthly performance summaries.
- Organized transaction records.

## Technology

- LibreOffice Calc
- Spreadsheet formulas
- Sample business data

## Project Status

Planned.

## Objective

Make everyday financial record-keeping easier through a straightforward spreadsheet solution.
